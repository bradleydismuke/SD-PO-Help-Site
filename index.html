NSDA default: 3-minute speech, then 2 minutes of questioning (direct questions, ~30 sec each recommended). Clock turns yellow at 30 seconds remaining, red when over. The PO may allow a grace period of a few seconds to finish a sentence per local rules. Beeps at end.

Precedence & Recency

Precedence: the legislator (not the school) with the fewest speeches on the legislation recognized first. Recency: among equals, the one who spoke longest ago. Authors/sponsors give the first speech on each item, then speeches alternate affirmative/negative. Always check your tournament rules (some track by session, some by legislation).

PO Score Sheet Reminders

Presiding officers are generally ranked on these. Confirm with your judging rubric.

  • Efficiency: keep the chamber moving; recognize speakers quickly, no dead time.
  • Fairness: accurate precedence/recency, consistent treatment of every member.
  • Knowledge of procedure: correct rulings, correct vote thresholds, proper motion order.
  • Command & poise: confident, courteous, clear voice; use "the chair" language.
  • Records: accurate speech counts, questioning times, and a recorded vote on every motion.
